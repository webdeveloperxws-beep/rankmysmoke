Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Sep 10, 2024

Rank My Smoke Store (“Rank My Smoke Store,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit rankmysmokestore.com, submit a form, book a consultation, communicate with us, or use our website development, SEO, Google Business Profile, social media, branding, reputation management, email marketing, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and job title.
  • Business or store name.
  • Email address and phone numbers.
  • Business address, city, and state.
  • Website or Google Maps link.
  • Number of store locations.
  • Project requirements, budget, and marketing goals.
  • Messages, files, approvals, and support requests.
  • Billing and transaction information.
  • Account credentials or platform access you provide for an approved project.

*Please do not send sensitive personal information through a general website form.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website.
  • Referring website or traffic source.
  • Approximate location.
  • Form interactions and website performance data.
  • Cookie and similar technology identifiers.

Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from scheduling platforms, analytics providers, payment processors, social media platforms, advertising or lead-generation partners, and other business tools used to operate our services.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Review your website, SEO, and Google Business Profile.
  • Prepare customized proposals and service recommendations.
  • Deliver and manage contracted services.
  • Process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Communicate about projects, approvals, reports, and invoices.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Protect our website and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting, and contractual obligations.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, understand visitor behavior, and improve website performance.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function. Others may support analytics, embedded content, or marketing. You can also control cookies through your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Analytics and performance-monitoring providers.
  • Scheduling, CRM, and communication platforms.
  • Payment processors and accounting providers.
  • Contractors working under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal, regulatory, or government authorities where required.
  • Professional advisers, such as attorneys and accountants.
  • A buyer or successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. If any use of advertising or analytics technology is considered “selling” or “sharing” under an applicable privacy law, eligible users may exercise the rights provided by that law.

5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We generally do not directly store complete payment card or banking information. Payment providers process that information under their own privacy policies and security practices.

6. Marketing Communications

If you subscribe to our communications, we may send service updates, marketing information, or relevant business content.

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails using the link provided in the email. Transactional communications about proposals, projects, payments, and active services may still be sent when necessary.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your inquiry.
  • Deliver contracted services.
  • Maintain business and payment records.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce agreements.
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements.

*Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the reason it was collected.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

No website, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security. Where the client chooses or requests the use of third-party services, including payment gateways, plugins, applications, hosting providers, API’s, or other external integrations, the security and data-handling practices of those services are governed by the respective third-party providers. We do not guarantee security, as these are outside of our control.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Request information about how your data is used or disclosed.
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications.
  • Opt out of qualifying sales or sharing of personal information.
  • Appeal a decision relating to a privacy request, where applicable.

To submit a request, email contact@rankmysmokestore.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. Authorized agents may be required to provide evidence of their authority and designation.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are designed for businesses and adults. They are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

If you believe that an individual under 18 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at contact@rankmysmokestore.com so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. Business Transfers

If Rank My Smoke Store is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.

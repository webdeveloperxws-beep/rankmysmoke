Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: Nov 07, 2025

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how refunds, project cancellations, and recurring service cancellations are handled by Rank My Smoke Store.

The terms of an approved proposal or signed service agreement will control if they differ from this policy.

1. One-Time Projects

One-time projects may include website design, website development, ecommerce setup, branding, audits, business profile setup, campaign setup, and other defined deliverables.

Before Work Begins

If you cancel before work begins, payments may be refunded after deducting:

  • Payment-processing fees.
  • Non-refundable third-party purchases.
  • Approved consultation or discovery charges.
  • Other costs already incurred for your project.

After Work Begins

Once discovery, research, strategy, design, development, setup, or production has started, payments covering completed work and reserved resources are non-refundable.

If a project is cancelled before completion, we will calculate the amount earned based on:

  • Work completed.
  • Time spent.
  • Milestones reached.
  • Resources reserved.
  • Third-party costs incurred.

Any balance due for completed work must be paid before files or deliverables are transferred.

2. Deposits and Initial Payments

An initial payment reserves production capacity and authorizes us to begin work. Once work begins, that payment is non-refundable unless the proposal expressly states otherwise.

Deposits cannot automatically be transferred to a different service or client without written approval.

3. Recurring Services

Recurring services may include SEO, local SEO, Google Business Profile management, website maintenance, social media management, reputation management, content, email marketing, and reporting.

Unless your signed agreement states otherwise:

  • Recurring services are billed in advance.
  • Cancellation requires at least 30 days’ written notice.
  • Cancellation becomes effective at the end of the applicable paid billing period.
  • Fees already charged for the active billing period are not prorated.
  • Work will continue through the paid period unless access is removed or the parties agree otherwise.

Any minimum commitment or fixed contract term will be stated in your proposal before payment.

4. Renewal and Cancellation

We will disclose whether a service renews automatically before you purchase it.

To cancel, email contact@rankmysmokestore.com with:

  • Your name.
  • Business name.
  • Service being cancelled.
  • Requested cancellation date.
  • Reason for cancellation, if you wish to provide one.

We will confirm receipt and the effective cancellation date in writing.

5. Third-Party Costs

The following are generally non-refundable once purchased or activated:

  • Domain registrations and renewals.
  • Website hosting.
  • Premium themes.
  • Plugins and software licenses.
  • Stock images, fonts, or media.
  • Directory, publisher, or placement fees.
  • Platform subscriptions.
  • Transaction and payment-processing fees.
  • Other third-party services approved for the project.

These services are governed by the third party’s own terms and refund rules.

6. Completed and Approved Work

Refunds are not available for:

  • Completed services.
  • Approved milestones.
  • Published content.
  • Launched websites.
  • Delivered files.
  • Completed audits or strategies.
  • Work delayed by missing client information or approval.
  • Dissatisfaction based solely on rankings, traffic, inquiries, or revenue when no guaranteed result was included in writing.

We will correct material errors within our agreed scope. A correction is not the same as a redesign or a new service request.

7. Project Delays and Inactivity

If a project is delayed because the client does not provide content, credentials, feedback, or approval, the project may be paused or rescheduled.

If no response is received for 30 days, we may classify the project as inactive. Restarting an inactive project may require:

  • Payment of outstanding invoices.
  • A restart fee.
  • A revised timeline.
  • A new proposal if the scope or platform has changed.

Inactivity caused by the client does not create a right to a refund.

8. Missed Consultations

Consultations may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' prior notice.

Missed appointments or late cancellations may be treated as used if preparation time was reserved or the consultation was a paid service.

9. Service Issues

If you believe we have not delivered an agreed service, contact us within 7 business days of the relevant delivery or billing date.

Your notice should identify:

  • The service involved.
  • The applicable proposal or invoice.
  • The specific issue.
  • The resolution requested.

We will review the request against the approved scope and available project records.

10. Chargeback and Payment Disputes

We encourage you to contact us before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute so we can investigate the charge and provide any relevant agreement, invoice, approval, communication, and service or delivery records.

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable rights available under applicable law.

11. Approved Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will generally be sent through the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

Approved refunds do not include non-refundable third-party expenses or processing charges where legally permitted.
